# ERE public-rule refresh and desk-reject attack, 2026-10-03

Sources reopened during this audit:
https://link.springer.com/journal/10640/aims-and-scope
and https://link.springer.com/journal/10640/submission-guidelines .
Public official guidance is evidence; historical repository PASS is not.

ERE accepts theoretical and applied environmental/resource economics.
Its stated priorities favor policy-oriented empirical research. A correct
theory paper therefore still needs a sufficiently substantive environmental
contribution; journal fit is not certified by algebra or Lean.

Journal-specific figure-legend placement controls over the publisher's
generic figure-placement wording. Unpublished-paper body mentions follow
the journal's published/accepted-only bibliography wording. The source
ZIP includes the new figure-legends file.

## Three strongest desk-reject cases and repairs

| Attack | Why it remains plausible | Concrete repair and surviving scope |
|---|---|---|
| Incremental contribution absorbed by protection/crowd-out and fiscal competition | Main ingredients are established; generic overprovision is not new | Added Friehe/Mungan, Choi/Bae/Jun, and Hirte et al.; explicitly assigned known mechanisms to precursors; retained only the particular endogenous-readiness policy comparison |
| Environmental content too reduced-form | Disaster probability is the protective technology; household/life/ecosystem benefits are absent | Introduction, welfare interpretation, and cover letter present an industrial component of place-based adaptation appraisal, not a general case against protection |
| Result too parameter-specific and portability mechanical | Exact result is an open existence result within symmetric primitives; two alternative specifications match first-order inputs by construction | Retained exact witness/open-set domain, disclosed full diagonal derivative and all strict margins; alternatives remain numerical diagnostics, with constructional logistic equality explained |

These edits improve interpretability and honesty; they cannot establish
ERE's discretionary novelty/significance threshold. Calibration, broader
incidence/heterogeneity, empirical application, and first-best instruments
would be a separate research program, not an authorized last-minute
expansion. No new economic mechanism or main theorem was added.
