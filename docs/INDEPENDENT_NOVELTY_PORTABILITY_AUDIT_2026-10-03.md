## Parent theorem specialization attempts

The application-neutral candidate has two public-policy players, mobile
duopolists, a unique endogenous private-preparedness continuation, and
private-information location. Its target is a negative national industrial
value of public protection on the full policy square together with a
positive symmetric policy Nash equilibrium and strict open-set persistence.
Standard contraction, monotonicity, concavity, and IFT results are proof
tools, not novel mathematical theorems.

The broad statement "public protection crowds out private protection and can
be excessive" is **PARTIALLY ABSORBED** and is not a main novelty claim.
The marginal identity under half incidence is an accounting/strategic
decomposition, not a new general fiscal-competition theorem.
No inspected parent supplied the particular full-domain policy ranking as
a short parameter restriction preserving its payoff primitives.
The strongest retained claim is the specific adaptation appraisal implication:
within this symmetric model family, endogenous readiness can make coordinated
additional protection globally undesirable while attraction supports a
positive globally incentive-compatible symmetric policy equilibrium.

**Decision:** retain that scoped application/theory result. Do not advertise
a new general crowd-out theorem, a new general infrastructure-competition
mechanism, or competitive-market robustness. No newly discovered source
invalidated the previously scoped maximum claim, so no scientific v5 is
created. The related-literature text now acknowledges the three new
published precursors. Unpublished Zhao/Yang/Zhang, Capponi/Du/Stiglitz,
and Castro-Vincenzi et al. remain named in text with working-paper links,
rather than listed as published/accepted references.

## Stage 7.5A: independently reassessed portability

**CONDITIONALLY PORTABLE**, with the exact theorem restricted to baseline.

For nonlinear risk \(q(a)=q_0e^{-a/q_0}\), both q(0)=q0 and q'(0)=-1
match by construction. The origin policy derivatives depend only on these
first-order inputs, so matching origin MS and ML is mechanical.
Curvature and finite deviations differ. Independent re-solving gives
alpha approximately 0.010410747 rather than baseline 0.023019685.

For logistic fit with scale H/2, L(0)=1/2 and L'(0)=1/(2H), matching
the uniform density at the symmetric cutoff. At symmetric policies D=0;
implicit differentiation gives
\(p_{a_A}=L'(0)D_{a_A}/[1-L'(0)D_p]\), exactly the uniform response
at that history. Since the symmetric surplus derivative with respect
to p vanishes, the symmetric policy FOC matches over the symmetric path,
not only at the origin. The equal alpha is therefore not independent
distributional robustness. Off-diagonal re-solving and full-domain
deviation searches are additional numerical evidence only.

No exact global alternative-family certificate or parameter-open
