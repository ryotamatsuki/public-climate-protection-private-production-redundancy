## 1. Canonical application-neutral representation

Strip the climate, plant, and jurisdiction labels.

### Downstream availability / investment game

Two firms choose continuous actions (r_i\in[0,1]) in a strictly concave quadratic payoff game. On the interior branch,

[
k r_i = A_i + h(1-r_j),
]

with (h=\Delta J>0), so the best-response slope is negative. This is a two-player linear-quadratic strategic-substitutes game with box constraints.

**Classification:** standard parent class. No novelty is claimed for strategic substitution, the linear system, or the interior fixed point.

### Endogenous discrete location game

Each firm chooses one of two locations after observing a private additive location-fit shock. Continuation-profit differences generate an affine cutoff probability under the uniform-shock normalization,

[
p=BR(p)=u_0+Bp.
]

Endpoint interiority implies no clipping and (|B|<1), giving a unique cutoff-probability equilibrium.

**Classification:** standard binary-choice Bayesian / affine fixed-point structure. No novelty is claimed for the cutoff architecture or contraction argument.

### First-stage decentralized policy game

After solving the downstream game, write national continuation surplus as (mathcal S(a_A,a_B)) and the probability that a mobile primary plant chooses jurisdiction (A) as (p(a_A,a_B)). Then

[
G_A(a_A,a_B)=\frac12\mathcal S(a_A,a_B)+2b p(a_A,a_B)-\frac c2 a_A^2,
]

while

[
W(a_A,a_B)=\mathcal S(a_A,a_B)+2b-\frac c2(a_A^2+a_B^2).
]

At the symmetric origin, symmetry alone gives

[
M_L=\frac14 M_S+2b\lambda_0.
]

**Classification:** this decomposition is an accounting/decentralization identity once the continuation maps are given. It is **not** retained as theorem novelty.

### Full coupled game

The nonstandard object is the endogenous composition of:

1. public protection changing primary disruption risk;
2. disruption risk changing firms' costly geographic backup choices;
3. backup choices changing disaster-state product-market availability and surplus;
4. protection changing mobile primary-plant location;
5. local governments valuing plant attraction while the planner does not;
6. the continuation response being strong enough that common protection has negative national marginal value while unilateral local protection retains positive value;
7. exact global certification that the planner optimum is ((0,0)) and the decentralized game has a positive symmetric Nash equilibrium on a nonempty open set.

This is the object subjected to theorem-level absorption below.

## 4. Known-model-in-disguise hostile attack

### Attack A — “This is just local public-input competition”

If private redundancy is fixed, yes: the model collapses toward the standard local-public-input competition family. This is now treated as a nested benchmark, not a contribution.

The attack fails against the headline theorem because the planner's national marginal value is itself altered by firms' endogenous geographic redundancy. The standard mobile-firm public-input models identified in the search do not supply that continuation response.

### Attack B — “This is just the safe-development paradox”

The safe-development literature already contains the broad idea that protection can attract development/exposure and reduce private preparedness. That broad mechanism is therefore not novel.

The attack still does not absorb the headline theorem. The present result requires strategic local governments competing for mobile oligopolistic production, firms choosing geographic backup for disaster-state production, and the resulting national/local policy-ordering conflict. The safe-development papers located do not provide a parent theorem from which the global planner-zero / local-positive equilibrium follows by direct specialization.

### Attack C — “This is just a generic externality / fiscal-competition wedge”

The marginal identity is generic and is explicitly downgraded. However, the headline theorem is not obtained by assuming a negative externality. The sign reversal is generated inside the model from the private backup response, and the global result additionally requires all downstream continuations, planner monotonicity, local concavity, root isolation, and best-response globality.

No searched general theorem was found that, after a short parameter substitution, produces the paper's full global result.

### Attack D — “The LQ backup game is the theory”

Rejected as a novelty route. The backup game is a standard strategic-substitutes quadratic game and is retained only as a mechanism block.

## 6. Surviving contribution

The maximum defensible novelty statement is:

> In a strategic two-jurisdiction mobile-oligopoly model, public protection endogenously crowds out firms' geographic backup readiness and changes primary-plant location. The interaction can make additional common protection reduce coordinated industrial surplus while the plant-attraction motive keeps unilateral local protection privately valuable; exact global certification then yields a nonempty open set with planner optimum zero and a strictly positive symmetric decentralized protection equilibrium.

This statement survives the parent-class mapping because no identified prior theorem jointly supplies the endogenous resilience continuation and the decentralized plant-attraction game from which the global policy-ranking reversal follows as a direct specialization or short corollary.

## 7. Strongest remaining novelty threat

The strongest threat is the **combination of the safe-development-paradox literature with the classic local-public-input competition literature**. Together they contain the two central intuitions separately.

Under v2.2 this does **not** amount to theorem absorption unless one prior theorem/model, or a direct general parent theorem, reproduces the complete strategic architecture or makes the headline result an immediate corollary. The audit did not identify such a theorem.

This means the paper must continue to avoid “first to show public protection crowds out private adaptation” or “first to show protection attracts activity” language. The novelty must remain attached to the full-game policy-ranking theorem.
