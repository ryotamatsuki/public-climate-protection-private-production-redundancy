\section*{Figure Legends}

\noindent\textbf{Fig.~\ref{fig:policy-regime}} Origin marginal incentives and product substitutability. The solid curve is $M_S(\gamma)$ and the dash-dotted curve is $M_L(\gamma)$ at zero additional protection, with the other primitives fixed at \eqref{eq:witness}. The dotted vertical lines mark their root-isolation thresholds $\gamma_L$ and $\gamma_S$. Between those thresholds the national marginal is negative and the local marginal is positive. These curves report marginal incentives at the origin; equilibrium policies and their boundaries are not plotted
