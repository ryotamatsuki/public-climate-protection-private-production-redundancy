\section{Mechanism Benchmarks}\label{sec:benchmarks}

The headline ranking does not require every feature of the oligopoly block. The following exact benchmark separates the availability-surplus distortion from incremental disaster-state market power.

\begin{proposition}[Conflict with no incremental sole-survivor rent]\label{prop:zero-delta}
In the same model family, take
\begin{equation}
(\gamma,q_0,k,H,b,c,\bar a)
=\left(0,\frac3{10},\frac2{25},\frac1{100},\frac{11}{200},10,\frac2{25}\right).
\label{eq:zero-delta-witness}
\end{equation}
Then $\Delta=0$, backup choices do not interact strategically, and primary-failure correlation does not affect additive product-market surplus. Nevertheless, the coordinated protection-policy optimum is uniquely $(0,0)$, and $(1/126,1/126)$ is a positive symmetric policy Nash equilibrium with globally optimal unilateral responses.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At $\gamma=0$, each product retains its own monopoly price distortion, but an unavailable rival creates no additional profit. For one firm, readiness, continuation profit, and surplus net of readiness cost are
\begin{equation}
r(q)=\frac{q}{4k},\quad
f(q)=\frac{1-q}{4}+\frac{q^2}{32k},\quad
t(q)=\frac38(1-q)+\frac{q^2}{16k}.
\end{equation}
Hence
\begin{equation}
p=\frac12+\frac{f(q_A)-f(q_B)}{2H},\qquad
\mathcal S=2\{pt(q_A)+(1-p)t(q_B)\}.
\end{equation}
Throughout the policy square, $11/16\leq r\leq15/16$ and $5/16\leq p\leq11/16$; the backup choices and location continuation are unique and interior. Substitution in the government and planner objectives gives
\begin{equation}
M_S=-\frac3{16},\qquad M_L=\frac5{128},\qquad
G_{A,a_A}(a,a)=\frac5{128}-\frac{315}{64}a.
\end{equation}
The last expression has the unique root $\alpha=1/126$. The policy functions here are polynomials. On the full square their exact Bernstein coefficients bound both planner partial derivatives between $-497/640$ and $-7/128$, so $(0,0)$ is uniquely optimal. Against $a_B=\alpha$, the coefficients of $G_{A,a_Aa_A}$ on the full own-policy interval lie between $-21726685/4064256$ and $-18154585/4064256$. Strict concavity makes $\alpha$ the global best response, including against boundary actions; symmetry gives the Nash claim. The accompanying benchmark script derives these polynomial bounds directly.
\end{proof}

This is a distinct witness: $k$ and $c$ differ from \eqref{eq:witness}, maintaining interiority when $\gamma=0$. It is not a claim that setting only $\gamma$ to zero at the canonical parameters preserves the continuation. Nor is it a competitive-price benchmark: own-product markups remain. What is removed is the incremental sole-survivor profit and the strategic backup substitution. At $\gamma=0$, the social--private readiness wedge in \eqref{eq:readiness-wedge} equals $s_i/8>0$. That uninternalized availability benefit is enough to generate the ranking when plant attraction is valuable.

Two further restricted comparisons clarify which response is being removed. In the \emph{fixed-primary-location} comparison, a primary-location profile or distribution is specified exogenously and kept fixed under all policy deviations; backup is re-solved. The plant-count derivative, and hence the attraction term, vanishes. In the \emph{fixed-private-readiness} comparison, each profile's readiness levels are held at specified constants while location is re-solved; the readiness-response term vanishes. These observations identify margins. Without specifying the fixed profile distribution or readiness values and solving the restricted policy games, they do not establish the absence of every policy-ranking conflict in those games.
