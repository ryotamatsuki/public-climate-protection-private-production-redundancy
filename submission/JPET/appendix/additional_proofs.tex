\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:global-primitive-band}: primitive sufficient conditions}\label{app:jpet-additional-proof-1}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:analytic-global} follows by substituting
$r(q)=q/(4k)$ and the location profit difference
$f(q_A)-f(q_B)=h(q)(x-y)$ into the policy objectives.
The first strict inequalities imply interior, unique private
continuations over the full policy square.
Direct differentiation gives
\begin{align*}
\mathcal S_{xx}
&=\frac1{16k}+2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4}-2U'd,\\
\mathcal S_{xy}
&=\frac1{16k}-2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4},\\
\mathcal S_{yy}
&=\frac1{16k}+2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4}+2U'd.
\end{align*}
The first cost bound implies strict negative diagonal dominance
of the symmetric Hessian $\nabla^2 W=\nabla^2\mathcal S-cI$
everywhere on the policy square, hence strict concavity.
At the zero-policy profile the gradient is
$\nabla W(0,0)=(-\mu_0,-\mu_0)$, with $\mu_0>0$
by $q_0>3k$. Strict concavity on the convex policy
square gives, for every $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$ with
$x,y\ge0$,
\[
W(x,y)< W(0,0)+\nabla W(0,0)\cdot(x,y)
= W(0,0)-\mu_0(x+y)<W(0,0).
\]
Therefore $(0,0)$ is the unique global coordinated
optimum. This supporting-hyperplane proof needs no
additional off-diagonal boundary-derivative condition.

The second cost bound ensures $G_{A,xx}<0$ everywhere, because
$\partial^2(2bp)/\partial x^2=b/(16Hk)$.
At $(x,y)=(a,a)$, direct differentiation yields
$G_{A,x}=m-sa$. Condition~\eqref{eq:analytic-band-d}
places $m/s$ in the interior; strict own-policy concavity proves
it is a global best response, and symmetry gives Nash equilibrium.

Adding $\mu(x+y)$ changes no Hessian or private continuation.
At symmetry, the planner marginal becomes
$W_x(a,a)+\mu=\mu-\mu_0-t_ca$ and the local marginal becomes
$m+\mu-sa$. Their unique interior roots are
\eqref{eq:analytic-policy-pair}, and the same strict global
concavity establishes their global optimality.
At $\mu=\mu_0$, the planner root is zero and the local root
is positive. Moreover, $1/s-1/t_c$ equals the positive expression
in \eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope} when $b>H$.
Thus the local optimum strictly exceeds the coordinated level
throughout the stated interval. At the rational witness,
\[
U_-=25/128,\quad U_+=325/512,\quad
D=1875/128,\quad D_2=15625/64.
\]
The sufficient cost bounds are $925/128$ and $3625/512$,
both below $c=10$. Moreover $\mu_0=3/32>0$ and
the location-interiority bound is $1/160<H=1/100$,
and $m/s=1/126$. All inequalities are strict.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:positive-gamma-extension}: positive substitutability}\label{app:jpet-additional-proof-2}

\begin{proof}
At $\gamma=0$, the full policy square has uniform strict
interiority margins for both private readiness $r$ and the
Bayesian location probability $p$. The analytic conditions in
Proposition~\ref{prop:global-primitive-band} also give strict
negative definiteness of the coordinated Hessian and strict
own-policy concavity for local governments, uniformly across
the compact policy square.

The differentiated Cournot payoffs and the backup first-order
system \eqref{eq:backup-linear-system} are smooth in $\gamma$ near
zero. Their denominator is $k^2$ at $\gamma=0$, uniformly over
all policy profiles and location configurations. Furthermore,
the clipped backup best-response map is a contraction with
modulus $\Delta(\gamma)J/k$, which vanishes at $\gamma=0$.
Uniform continuity on the compact policy square therefore
ensures a unique, strictly interior, smooth private-readiness
continuation for every sufficiently small $\gamma>0$.

At $\gamma=0$, the location best-response cutoffs $u_0$
and $u_1$ in \eqref{eq:location-endpoints} coincide and
are uniformly inside $(0,1)$ for every policy profile.
Smoothness of the full profile-specific continuation
profits implies that both cutoffs remain interior for
sufficiently small $\gamma>0$, uniformly in first-stage policies.
Because $|u_1-u_0|<1$, the location best response remains a
contraction on $[0,1]$, including when asymmetric location
probabilities are allowed. The unique interior Bayesian
location continuation \eqref{eq:location-prob} is therefore
smooth in policies and $\gamma$.

The resulting $\mathcal S$, $W$, and $G_j$ are $C^2$ in the
policies and $\gamma$ near the whole compact policy square.
By continuity of Hessians and strict compact-domain margins,
the globally negative-definite planner Hessian and strictly
negative government own-policy curvature persist for all
sufficiently small positive $\gamma$.

At $\gamma=0,\mu=1/10$, the augmented planner's symmetric
stationary point and the symmetric government best-response
stationary point are distinct, strictly positive, and interior.
Both derivatives defining these points have strictly negative
slopes, so the implicit function theorem continues the
two stationary levels smoothly to $\gamma>0$.
Strict global concavity makes the planner point the unique
global optimum and the government point a global unilateral
best response; symmetry establishes Nash equilibrium.
The strict gap $a^N(0)>a^P(0)$ survives by continuity.

Finally, the strict comparative static in
\eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope} holds at $(0,1/10)$.
Smooth parameter dependence preserves its sign on a
sufficiently small open neighborhood, including $\gamma>0$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:benefit-gap-response-interaction}: three policy games}\label{app:jpet-additional-proof-3}

\begin{proof}
Part (i) follows from Proposition~
\ref{prop:positive-optimum-benchmark} and the exact
positive-gap derivative in
\eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope}, evaluated at the
maintained rational parameters.

For part (ii), the fixed-readiness continuation in
Proposition~\ref{prop:restricted-game-comparisons}
leaves each government's own-policy curvature equal
to $-10$ along the symmetric policy path, identical
to the planner's symmetric own-policy curvature.
The new linear benefit simply adds $\mu$ to each
first-order condition; thus the planner and local
stationary roots both rise at rate $1/10$.
The full-square planner Hessian remains strictly
negative definite, and each government's own-policy
second derivative is $-20405/2048<0$.
The roots therefore remain globally optimal.

For part (iii), the previously re-solved fixed-location
game has the exact partial derivatives
$W_{a_A}=-3/32-(135/16)a_A$ and
$G_{A,a_A}=-3/64-(295/32)a_A$.
Adding $\mu$ yields the roots displayed above.
Each objective is strictly concave in the relevant
policy coordinates over the entire policy square.
On the specified open interval, all six stated
policy levels are strictly between $0$ and
$\bar a=2/25$. Exact subtraction gives
$32/295-16/135=-16/1593$.
The independent exact scripts
\texttt{positive\_optimum\_exact.py} and
\texttt{restricted\_games\_exact.py}
regenerate the resulting comparisons.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:incidence-robustness}: regional incidence and hosting}\label{app:jpet-additional-proof-4}

\begin{proof}
At $(\gamma,\eta,\rho,\mu)=(0,0,0,1/10)$,
Propositions~\ref{prop:positive-optimum-benchmark},
\ref{prop:global-primitive-band}, and
\ref{prop:positive-gamma-extension} give uniform strict
continuation-interiority margins on the compact policy square,
a strictly negative-definite Hessian for the coordinated
planner, strict local own-policy concavity, two nondegenerate
strictly positive symmetric policy roots, a strict
decentralized--coordinated ranking, and a strictly positive
derivative of the policy-level gap with respect to $\mu$.

The primary-plant operation probabilities in
\eqref{eq:availability-conditional} are affine in
the protection choices and do not depend on backup operation.
The equilibrium Bayesian location probability and industrial
surplus are smooth in public policies and $\gamma$
in a neighborhood of the compact policy square by the
nonsingular private backup system and uniformly interior
location cutoffs. The new regional shares and hosting payoffs
are smooth in $\eta,\rho$ and these private continuations. Thus the modified local
payoffs and their sum are jointly $C^2$ in public policies
and parameters, with continuous higher derivatives needed
for the policy-level comparative static. Uniform strict
Hessian and interior margins therefore persist for
sufficiently small perturbations. The implicit function
theorem continues the positive symmetric planner and Nash
policy levels smoothly in $(\gamma,\eta,\rho,\mu)$.
Strict global planner concavity and strict local own-policy
concavity establish full-policy optimality, while
continuous dependence preserves the positive gap and its
strictly positive $\mu$ derivative.

For the final formula, set $\gamma=\rho=0$. On the
symmetric path,
\[
\frac{\partial G_A^{\eta,0}}{\partial a_A}(a,a)
=m+\mu-sa+\frac{\eta}{H}\,
 t(q_0-a)\,h(q_0-a),
\]
where $m,s$ are defined by \eqref{eq:analytic-parameters}.
At $\eta=0$, the equilibrium root is
$a^N=(\mu+m)/s$, and its derivative with
respect to the same common policy level is $-s<0$.
Implicit differentiation gives
$\partial_\eta a^N=t(q^N)h(q^N)/(Hs)$.
Substitution of the rational witness yields
\eqref{eq:incidence-response}. The coordinated planner's
policy is independent of $\eta$, since its two regional
surplus shares always sum to one.
\end{proof}



\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-gap-amplification}: product substitutability}
\label{app:jpet-additional-proof-5}

\begin{proof}
We differentiate the \emph{complete} private continuation
at $\gamma=0$, including its effects on backup, the
Bayesian location cutoff, and expected disaster-state welfare.
Write $\pi_D=(2+\gamma)^{-2}$,
$\Delta=\pi_M-\pi_D$, and denote derivatives at
$\gamma=0$ by a dot. Thus
$\pi_D(0)=1/4$, $\dot\pi_D=-1/4$,
$\Delta(0)=0$, and $\dot\Delta=1/4$.

For any one of the four primary-location profiles, let the
two marginal primary risks be $(s_1,s_2)$ and the joint
primary-failure probability be $J$. Differentiating the
backup system~\eqref{eq:backup-linear-system} at zero yields
\begin{equation}
r_i^0=\frac{s_i}{4k},\qquad
\dot r_i=\frac{-s_i+J(1-r_j^0)}{4k}.
\label{eq:gamma-r-first-order}
\end{equation}
The envelope theorem applied to the firm's equilibrium
continuation profit~\eqref{eq:expected-profit} gives
\begin{equation}
\dot\Pi_i
=-\frac14\bigl[1-s_i(1-r_i^0)\bigr]
+\frac14\left[
s_j(1-r_j^0)-J(1-r_i^0)(1-r_j^0)
\right].
\label{eq:gamma-private-profit}
\end{equation}
Let $m_i=s_i(1-r_i^0)$ and
$z=J(1-r_1^0)(1-r_2^0)$.
Since the duopoly material surplus is
$T_D=(3+\gamma)/(2+\gamma)^2$ and the
monopoly material surplus is $T_M=3/8$,
differentiating the disaster-state surplus net of
private readiness costs gives
\begin{equation}
\dot S^\ell
=-\frac12+\frac12(m_1+m_2-z)
+\frac18(s_1\dot r_1+s_2\dot r_2)
\label{eq:gamma-state-surplus}
\end{equation}
for each location profile $\ell$.

Let $d_A=\Pi_1^{AA}-\Pi_1^{BA}$ and
$d_B=\Pi_1^{AB}-\Pi_1^{BB}$, as in
Section~\ref{sec:equilibrium}.
At zero substitutability,
$p_0=1/2+[f(q_A)-f(q_B)]/(2H)$.
Differentiating the Bayesian equilibrium cutoff~\eqref{eq:location-prob}
gives the \emph{off-diagonal} response
\begin{equation}
\dot p=\frac{p_0\dot d_A+(1-p_0)\dot d_B}{2H},
\label{eq:gamma-location-derivative}
\end{equation}
where $\dot d_A,\dot d_B$ use
\eqref{eq:gamma-private-profit} for the corresponding
location profiles. The $\gamma$ derivative of expected
national industrial surplus, after re-solving the entire
location continuation, is
\begin{align}
\dot{\mathcal S}
={}&p_0^2\dot S^{AA}
+2p_0(1-p_0)\dot S^{AB}
+(1-p_0)^2\dot S^{BB}
\nonumber\\
&+2\dot p\,[t(q_A)-t(q_B)].
\label{eq:gamma-national-surplus}
\end{align}
The last line captures the change in location choice and
cannot be discarded before differentiating with respect to
the unilateral government action.

With the exact primitives of the proposition, set
\[
F_P^\gamma(a)=
\left.\partial_{a_A}\dot{\mathcal S}
\right|_{a_A=a_B=a},
\quad
F_N^\gamma(a)=
\left.\left(
\frac12\partial_{a_A}\dot{\mathcal S}
+2b\partial_{a_A}\dot p
\right)\right|_{a_A=a_B=a}.
\]
Substitution of \eqref{eq:gamma-r-first-order}--
\eqref{eq:gamma-national-surplus} gives the exact
polynomials
\begin{align*}
F_P^\gamma(a)
&=-\frac{3(50000a^3-62500a^2+39040a-4877)}
        {20480},\\
F_N^\gamma(a)
&=\frac{
687500000a^5+316250000a^4-129450000a^3+
7873000a^2-9964970a+970381
}{655360}.
\end{align*}
At $\gamma=0$, the augmented coordinated and local
symmetric first-stage conditions have slopes
$-t_c$ and $-s$, respectively, with
$t_c=c-1/(8k)$ and $s=c-(H+b)/(16Hk)$.
By the implicit function theorem and full-domain
strict concavity already established in
Proposition~\ref{prop:positive-gamma-extension},
\[
\left.\frac{da^P}{d\gamma}\right|_{0^+}
=\frac{F_P^\gamma(1/1350)}{t_c},
\qquad
\left.\frac{da^N}{d\gamma}\right|_{0^+}
=\frac{F_N^\gamma(89/3150)}{s}.
\]
The displayed exact rational expressions in
\eqref{eq:gamma-gap-amplification} follow by
substitution and subtraction. All three derivatives
are strictly positive. Smoothness implies that their
strict signs persist in a sufficiently small
nonnegative-$\gamma$ neighborhood.
The independent \texttt{gamma\_policy\_derivative\_exact.py}
reconstructs these formulas from the original
state-contingent continuation primitives.
\end{proof}


\subsection{Exact global certificates for the benchmark propositions}
\label{app:jpet-benchmark-certs}

For Proposition~\ref{prop:zero-delta}, the continuation is interior on
$D=[0,2/25]^2$. The exact Bernstein coefficient bounds for the
planner's policy marginals are
\[
-\frac{497}{640}\leq W_{a_A},W_{a_B}\leq-\frac7{128}<0.
\]
With $\alpha=1/126$, the own-policy second derivative of the
local government's objective, evaluated for all feasible unilateral
deviations against $a_B=\alpha$, satisfies
\[
-\frac{21726685}{4064256}\leq G_{A,a_Aa_A}
\leq-\frac{18154585}{4064256}<0.
\]
Thus the planner objective is strictly decreasing in each policy
coordinate and the local policy objective is strictly concave in its
own action against the candidate equilibrium. These whole-domain
claims imply the globally optimal policies asserted in the proposition.
The exact polynomial bounds are regenerated by
\texttt{scripts/verify\_benchmarks.py}.

\begin{align*}
-\frac{2485}{256}
&\leq\widetilde W_{a_Aa_A},\widetilde W_{a_Ba_B}

The first two inequalities give a uniformly negative-definite
