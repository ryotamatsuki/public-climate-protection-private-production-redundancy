## Most plausible original contribution
A **specific full-game joint policy-ranking**: primary-risk-reducing public inputs lower privately selected backup readiness, making marginal industrial protection value negative even though engineering value is positive; mobile firms still induce positive local policies that survive every unilateral deviation. Global certificates and exact ablations show it is an actual equilibrium, not merely simultaneous local first-order signs.

## Strongest remaining objection
Published fiscal-competition overprovision + private prevention substitution + private resilience wedge may make this result an expected rather than independently insightful coupling. The relevance of the global existence certificate alone to a JPET theory reader is not yet definitively established.

Bayindir-Upmann (1998) full published theorem text was not available. The 1995 preprint is indexed but the repository retrieval blocked. Do not say that parent theorem has been ruled out or that JPET R&R is guaranteed. The explicit analytical proof and code cannot establish editorial novelty.
