## Most plausible original contribution
A **specific full-game joint policy-ranking**: primary-risk-reducing public inputs lower privately selected backup readiness, making marginal industrial protection value negative even though engineering value is positive; mobile firms still induce positive local policies that survive every unilateral deviation. Global certificates and exact ablations show it is an actual equilibrium, not merely simultaneous local first-order signs.
