# Round 0 — Referee 1 (public-economics novelty)
**Simulated independent review, 2026-11-05; NOT an actual JPET referee report.**

## Summary
The paper establishes an existence result in a two-jurisdiction public-protection game with mobile firms, endogenous backup readiness, and state-contingent Cournot production: the coordinated policy optimum is zero while positive symmetric decentralized protection survives global unilateral-deviation checks. The main local-versus-social derivative decomposition is incidence arithmetic; the economic effect is a negative industrial public-protection margin induced by inefficiently low private backup readiness.

## Strengths
(1) Policy-constrained planner and industrial-welfare boundary explicitly defined; (2) exact global equilibrium certificates rather than on-path FOCs alone; (3) exact delta-zero benchmark separates incremental sole-survivor rents from the policy-ranking effect.

## Major concerns (severity)
- **N1 / potentially fatal**: Overprovision of industrial public inputs is already studied by Bayindir-Upmann (1998), Walz and Wellisch (1996), Maurer and Walz (2000), and Billon (2023). The particular *combination* is not by itself novel. Need a primitive-to-theorem absorption analysis, including direct corollary attempts, and a one-paragraph statement of the residual economic result.
- **N2 / major**: A model-specific existence theorem supported by a canonical witness may not by itself explain an economically important region of behavior. Establish transparent primitive or qualitative conditions, or demonstrate meaningful additional predictions beyond existence.
- **M2 / major**: Because the same planner-zero/local-positive ranking holds at delta=0, explain what the richer differentiated-product oligopoly adds beyond the separable baseline; do not imply monopoly-rent wedges are necessary.
- **L1 / major**: Bayindir-Upmann (1998) full published text was not accessible in this session. The 1995 Bielefeld preprint is listed, but its repository blocks automated access; mark direct theorem comparison UNRESOLVED. Billon (2023) is available as full HTML. DOI metadata checked. Do not promote lack of full text to a novelty PASS.

## Most damaging objection
Standard private/public self-protection substitution plus standard interjurisdictional public-input rivalry could rationalize the two signs. Does the model deliver a non-obvious sign restriction or general characterization that follows from neither parent, or just exhibit the expected interaction using exact certificates?

## Recommendation
**BELOW R&R / Reject on importance risk at current revision.** Reconsider after a theorem-absorption matrix and a targeted mechanism/robustness result; a new theorem is not compulsory, but improved writing alone is unlikely to resolve N2.
