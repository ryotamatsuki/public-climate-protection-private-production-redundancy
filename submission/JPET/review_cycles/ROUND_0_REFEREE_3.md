# Round 0 — Referee 3 (economic mechanism and welfare)
**Simulated adversarial report, not an actual referee. 2026-11-05.**

## Summary and merits
The decomposition distinguishes engineering benefits from readiness responses after real backup costs are included. The $Δ=0$ benchmark demonstrates that scarcity rents and strategic backup substitution are not required for the planner-zero/local-positive ranking. The model is transparent about the constrained planner and omitted nonindustrial benefits.

## Main objections
1. **W1 / major**. A social optimum of exactly zero is very sensitive to excluding public protection benefits for lives, housing, and other assets. Even a small positive direct benefit may raise the planner optimum above zero. The paper is honest about this, but JPET readers need a transparent positive-planner-optimum case, provided it is correctly solved.
2. **M2 / major**. Government receives half of all industrial surplus and a location-attached $b$ independent of firm operation; neither is derived from local ownership and employment. The existing generalized-incidence remark proves only a local sign decomposition, not a global policy theorem. No generalized theorem should be claimed without full equilibrium re-solution.
3. **M1 / major**. Pure fiscal competition can generate overinvestment without private readiness adjustment. The claim must be: readiness crowd-out can lower (even reverse the sign of) the industrial social margin and can worsen competition's ranking, *not* that crowd-out invents interjurisdictional overprovision.
4. **M2 / major**. If positive public inputs are common in reality, a full-game extension with positive optimal investment and positive-but-excessive local provision would materially reduce the knife-edge narrative. Prefer a compact exact benchmark over broad numerical demonstrations.

## Counterfactuals required
- Fix backup readiness at documented values, reoptimize both planner and local-government policy over entire policy rectangle.
- Fix primary-location distribution, reoptimize readiness and policy. Avoid equating vanishing attraction margin with a complete welfare-ranking reversal unless checked.
- For each, report market effect, private incentive wedge, and total welfare under identical accounting.

## Recommendation
**Below R&R on interpretation/robustness, but fixable** with one exact positive-planner-optimum benchmark and restrained, explicit policy implications. Do not require an unrestricted general theorem.
