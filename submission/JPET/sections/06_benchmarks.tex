\section{Mechanism Benchmarks}\label{sec:benchmarks}

The headline ranking does not require every feature of the oligopoly block. The following exact benchmark separates the availability-surplus distortion from incremental disaster-state market power.

\begin{proposition}[Conflict with no incremental sole-survivor rent]\label{prop:zero-delta}
In the same model family, take
\begin{equation}
(\gamma,q_0,k,H,b,c,\bar a)
=\left(0,\frac3{10},\frac2{25},\frac1{100},\frac{11}{200},10,\frac2{25}\right).
\label{eq:zero-delta-witness}
\end{equation}
Then $\Delta=0$, backup choices do not interact strategically, and primary-failure correlation does not affect additive product-market surplus. Nevertheless, the coordinated protection-policy optimum is uniquely $(0,0)$, and $(1/126,1/126)$ is a positive symmetric policy Nash equilibrium with globally optimal unilateral responses.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At $\gamma=0$, each product retains its own monopoly price distortion, but an unavailable rival creates no additional profit. For one firm, readiness, continuation profit, and surplus net of readiness cost are
\begin{equation}
r(q)=\frac{q}{4k},\quad
f(q)=\frac{1-q}{4}+\frac{q^2}{32k},\quad
t(q)=\frac38(1-q)+\frac{q^2}{16k}.
\end{equation}
Hence
\begin{equation}
p=\frac12+\frac{f(q_A)-f(q_B)}{2H},\qquad
\mathcal S=2\{pt(q_A)+(1-p)t(q_B)\}.
\end{equation}
Throughout the policy square, $11/16\leq r\leq15/16$ and $5/16\leq p\leq11/16$; the backup choices and location continuation are unique and interior. Substitution in the government and planner objectives gives
\begin{equation}
M_S=-\frac3{16},\qquad M_L=\frac5{128},\qquad
G_{A,a_A}(a,a)=\frac5{128}-\frac{315}{64}a.
\end{equation}
The last expression has the unique root $\alpha=1/126$. The policy functions here are polynomials. On the full square their exact Bernstein coefficients bound both planner partial derivatives between $-497/640$ and $-7/128$, so $(0,0)$ is uniquely optimal. Against $a_B=\alpha$, the coefficients of $G_{A,a_Aa_A}$ on the full own-policy interval lie between $-21726685/4064256$ and $-18154585/4064256$. Strict concavity makes $\alpha$ the global best response, including against boundary actions; symmetry gives the Nash claim. The accompanying benchmark script derives these polynomial bounds directly.
\end{proof}

This is a distinct witness: $k$ and $c$ differ from \eqref{eq:witness}, maintaining interiority when $\gamma=0$. It is not a claim that setting only $\gamma$ to zero at the canonical parameters preserves the continuation. Nor is it a competitive-price benchmark: own-product markups remain. What is removed is the incremental sole-survivor profit and the strategic backup substitution. At $\gamma=0$, the social--private readiness wedge in \eqref{eq:readiness-wedge} equals $s_i/8>0$. That uninternalized availability benefit is enough to generate the ranking when plant attraction is valuable.

Two further restricted comparisons clarify which response is being removed. In the \emph{fixed-primary-location} comparison, a primary-location profile or distribution is specified exogenously and kept fixed under all policy deviations; backup is re-solved. The plant-count derivative, and hence the attraction term, vanishes. In the \emph{fixed-private-readiness} comparison, each profile's readiness levels are held at specified constants while location is re-solved; the readiness-response term vanishes. These observations identify margins. Without specifying the fixed profile distribution or readiness values and solving the restricted policy games, they do not establish the absence of every policy-ranking conflict in those games.


\subsection{Positive coordinated provision with decentralized overinvestment}
\label{sec:positive-planner-extension}

The preceding benchmark has a zero coordinated optimum because its policy objective
covers industrial surplus only. The conflict between coordinated and decentralized
policy levels is not logically tied to that corner. For a transparent sensitivity
comparison, consider an \emph{augmented welfare specification} in which the
protective investment in jurisdiction $j$ generates an additional direct local
benefit $\mu a_j$, valued identically by that government and the coordinated
planner. Keep the $\Delta=0$ primitives in \eqref{eq:zero-delta-witness};
the new objectives are
\begin{equation}
\widetilde G_j=G_j+\mu a_j,\qquad
\widetilde W=W+\mu(a_A+a_B).
\label{eq:augmented-direct-benefit}
\end{equation}
This is an extension of the welfare accounting, not a claim that the baseline
industrial planner values benefits to households or other assets.

\begin{proposition}[Positive coordinated protection and local overprovision]
\label{prop:positive-optimum-benchmark}
For every
\begin{equation}
\frac{3}{32}<\mu<\frac{227}{640},
\label{eq:mu-range}
\end{equation}
the unique global coordinated optimum of the augmented game on
$[0,\bar a]^2$ is the strictly positive symmetric profile
\begin{equation}
a^P_A=a^P_B=a^P=\frac{32\mu-3}{270}.
\end{equation}
The decentralized augmented game has a strictly positive symmetric Nash
equilibrium, with every unilateral policy deviation globally dominated,
at
\begin{equation}
a^N_A=a^N_B=a^N=\frac{128\mu+5}{630},
\end{equation}
and the local policy level strictly exceeds the coordinated optimum:
\begin{equation}
a^N-a^P=\frac{2(40\mu+9)}{945}>0.
\end{equation}
For example, at $\mu=1/10$ the coordinated optimum is $1/1350$ and
the symmetric local equilibrium is $89/3150$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
With $\gamma=0$, all private profit and readiness continuations are
unchanged by the additional government-valued direct benefit.
The exact continuation is given by \eqref{eq:zero-delta-witness}
and the continuation formulas stated in the proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:zero-delta}: $r(q)=q/(4k)$,
$f(q)=(1-q)/4+q^2/(32k)$, and
$t(q)=3(1-q)/8+q^2/(16k)$, with
$p=1/2+[f(q_A)-f(q_B)]/(2H)$.
The earlier global interiority bounds
$11/16\le r\le15/16$ and $5/16\le p\le11/16$
continue to apply on the whole policy square.

Substituting the same exact continuation into
\eqref{eq:augmented-direct-benefit} yields the symmetric-path
policy derivatives
\begin{equation}
\left.\frac{\partial\widetilde W}{\partial a_A}\right|_{(a,a)}
=\mu-\frac{3}{32}-\frac{135}{16}a,
\qquad
\left.\frac{\partial\widetilde G_A}{\partial a_A}\right|_{(a,a)}
=\mu+\frac{5}{128}-\frac{315}{64}a.
\label{eq:augmented-origin-policies}
\end{equation}
The first equation has the stated symmetric planner root $a^P$,
and the second has the stated symmetric local root $a^N$.
Condition \eqref{eq:mu-range} places both strictly inside
$(0,\bar a)$ and ensures $a^N>a^P$.

A root on the symmetric path alone is not sufficient for either claim.
Using the bivariate polynomial representations of $\widetilde W$
and $\widetilde G_A$ obtained from the exact private continuation,
tensor-product Bernstein coefficients on
$[0,2/25]^2$ give, uniformly and independently of $\mu$,
\begin{align*}
-\frac{2485}{256}
&\le \widetilde W_{a_Aa_A},
  \widetilde W_{a_Ba_B}
\le -\frac{1885}{256},\\
\frac{128925}{2048}
&\le
 \widetilde W_{a_Aa_A}\widetilde W_{a_Ba_B}
  -(\widetilde W_{a_Aa_B})^2
\le \frac{2607175}{32768},\\
-\frac{2845}{512}
&\le \widetilde G_{A,a_Aa_A}
\le-\frac{2245}{512}.
\end{align*}
The first two strict bounds imply that the planner's objective is
strictly concave on the entire policy square. By symmetry, its
symmetric stationary point is therefore the unique global optimum.
The final bound implies strict own-policy concavity for either
jurisdiction against any rival action. Consequently, the symmetric
local stationary point is a global best response against $a^N$
and thus a Nash equilibrium. The reported difference follows by
exact rational subtraction. The accompanying standalone SymPy
script regenerates these bivariate Bernstein coefficient bounds.
\end{proof}

This benchmark addresses the corner-optimum concern within the
separable-product version of the game: a positive social optimum and
strictly excessive local provision coexist. At $\mu=1/10$, removing
the location-attached hosting benefit by setting $b=0$ while leaving
all other primitives unchanged gives a positive symmetric local equilibrium
$a^{N,b=0}=17/2950$, between the planner optimum $1/1350$ and
the equilibrium with hosting $89/3150$. Thus positive-optimum
overprovision in this augmented game does not require plant attraction:
the hosting channel amplifies but does not uniquely cause that ranking.
This contrast holds under the same exact globally concave policy objectives.

It does \emph{not} establish
a positive coordinated optimum over the full differentiated-product
family, nor does it make public--private protection substitution or
public-input overprovision generally new. Moreover, with $\mu>3/32$
the augmented coordinated marginal at zero is positive: the
interpretation is \emph{overinvestment relative to a positive social
optimum}, not a negative total social marginal at the origin.
