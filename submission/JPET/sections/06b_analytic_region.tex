\section{A Primitive Region and a Differentiated-Product Extension}
\label{sec:analytic-region}

The global certificates above establish the principal policy conflict at
explicit parameter vectors. The separable-product case also permits an
analytical sufficient-condition region. More importantly, this region
permits a continuation argument establishing a positive coordinated
optimum with decentralized overprovision for genuinely positive product
substitutability.

For $\gamma=0$, let $x=a_A$, $y=a_B$, $d=x-y$,
$q=q_0-(x+y)/2$, and set
\begin{equation}
U(q)=\frac{1}{32k}+\frac{(4k-q)(3k-q)}{128Hk^2},
\qquad h(q)=\frac{4k-q}{16k}.
\label{eq:analytic-U}
\end{equation}
Writing $t(q)=3(1-q)/8+q^2/(16k)$, the complete private
continuation induces the \emph{global} identities
\begin{equation}
\mathcal S(x,y)=2t(q)+U(q)d^2,\qquad
p(x,y)=\frac12+\frac{h(q)d}{2H}.
\label{eq:analytic-global}
\end{equation}
Let $Q=[q_0-\bar a,q_0]$, and define
\[
U_-=\min_Q U,\quad U_+=\max_Q U,\quad
D=\max_Q|U'|,\quad D_2=(64Hk^2)^{-1}.
\]
Also define
\begin{equation}
m=\frac{H(3k-q_0)+b(4k-q_0)}{16Hk},\quad
s=c-\frac{H+b}{16Hk},\quad
t_c=c-\frac1{8k},\quad
\mu_0=\frac{q_0-3k}{8k}.
\label{eq:analytic-parameters}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Global primitive conditions and benefit-induced overprovision]
\label{prop:global-primitive-band}
Suppose the following strict conditions hold:
\begin{align}
&3k<q_0<4k,\quad 0<\bar a<q_0,\quad U_->0,\quad
(4k-q_0+\bar a)\bar a<16kH,
\label{eq:analytic-band-a}\\
&\mu_0>D\bar a^2/2,
\label{eq:analytic-band-b}\\
&c>\max\left\{
\frac{1}{8k}+4U_++2D\bar a+\frac{D_2\bar a^2}{2},
\frac12\left(\frac1{8k}+2U_++2D\bar a+
\frac{D_2\bar a^2}{4}\right)+\frac{b}{16Hk}
\right\},
\label{eq:analytic-band-c}\\
&m>0,\qquad 0<m/s<\bar a.
\label{eq:analytic-band-d}
\end{align}
Then the unaugmented game has a unique coordinated global optimum
$(0,0)$ and a strictly positive symmetric decentralized Nash
equilibrium $(m/s,m/s)$, with globally optimal unilateral responses
and unique interior private continuations throughout the policy square.

If additionally $b>H$ and
\begin{equation}
\mu_0<\mu<
\min\{\mu_0+t_c\bar a,\ s\bar a-m\},
\label{eq:analytic-mu-range}
\end{equation}
then the augmented-benefit game of
\eqref{eq:augmented-direct-benefit} has a strictly positive unique
coordinated optimum and a strictly larger symmetric Nash level,
both interior and globally incentive-compatible:
\begin{equation}
a^P=\frac{\mu-\mu_0}{t_c}
<a^N=\frac{\mu+m}{s}.
\label{eq:analytic-policy-pair}
\end{equation}
The overprovision gap increases with the directly valued benefit:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial(a^N-a^P)}{\partial\mu}
=\frac{b-H}{16Hk\,s\,t_c}>0.
\label{eq:analytic-gap-slope}
\end{equation}
This is a nonempty strict primitive region, containing the exact
rational benchmark \eqref{eq:zero-delta-witness} at $\mu=1/10$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:analytic-global} follows by substituting
$r(q)=q/(4k)$ and the location profit difference
$f(q_A)-f(q_B)=h(q)(x-y)$ into the policy objectives.
The first strict inequalities imply interior, unique private
continuations over the full policy square.
Direct differentiation gives
\begin{align*}
\mathcal S_{xx}
&=\frac1{16k}+2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4}-2U'd,\\
\mathcal S_{xy}
&=\frac1{16k}-2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4},\\
\mathcal S_{yy}
&=\frac1{16k}+2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4}+2U'd.
\end{align*}
The first cost bound implies strict negative diagonal dominance
of the symmetric Hessian $\nabla^2 W=\nabla^2\mathcal S-cI$
everywhere on the policy square, hence strict concavity.
At any $(0,y)$ the remaining marginal satisfies
\[
W_x(0,y)
=-\mu_0-\tfrac12U'(q_0-y/2)y^2-2U(q_0-y/2)y
\le-\mu_0+D\bar a^2/2<0.
\]
Because $W_{xx}<0$, $W_x(x,y)<0$ for $x>0$; the
symmetric argument applies to $W_y$. Thus $(0,0)$ is the
unique global coordinated optimum.

The second cost bound ensures $G_{A,xx}<0$ everywhere, because
$\partial^2(2bp)/\partial x^2=b/(16Hk)$.
At $(x,y)=(a,a)$, direct differentiation yields
$G_{A,x}=m-sa$. Condition~\eqref{eq:analytic-band-d}
places $m/s$ in the interior; strict own-policy concavity proves
it is a global best response, and symmetry gives Nash equilibrium.

Adding $\mu(x+y)$ changes no Hessian or private continuation.
At symmetry, the planner marginal becomes
$W_x(a,a)+\mu=\mu-\mu_0-t_ca$ and the local marginal becomes
$m+\mu-sa$. Their unique interior roots are
\eqref{eq:analytic-policy-pair}, and the same strict global
concavity establishes their global optimality.
At $\mu=\mu_0$, the planner root is zero and the local root
is positive. Moreover, $1/s-1/t_c$ equals the positive expression
in \eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope} when $b>H$.
Thus the local optimum strictly exceeds the coordinated level
throughout the stated interval. At the rational witness,
\[
U_-=25/128,\quad U_+=325/512,\quad
D=1875/128,\quad D_2=15625/64.
\]
The sufficient cost bounds are $925/128$ and $3625/512$,
both below $c=10$. Also $\mu_0=3/32>3/64=D\bar a^2/2$,
the location-interiority bound is $1/160<H=1/100$,
and $m/s=1/126$. All inequalities are strict.
\end{proof}


\subsection{An open region with positive product substitutability}

\begin{proposition}[Positive optimal protection and overprovision at $\gamma>0$]
\label{prop:positive-gamma-extension}
Fix the rational primitives of \eqref{eq:zero-delta-witness} and
let $\mu=1/10$. There exists $\epsilon>0$ such that for every
$\gamma\in(0,\epsilon)$, with product substitutability strictly
positive, the complete game admits unique interior private backup
and Bayesian location continuations for all first-stage policy
profiles. The augmented coordinated protection-policy planner has a
strictly positive, symmetric \emph{unique global optimum}
$a^P(\gamma)$. The decentralized game has a strictly positive
symmetric Nash equilibrium $a^N(\gamma)$, with globally optimal
unilateral responses, satisfying
\begin{equation}
0<a^P(\gamma)<a^N(\gamma)<\bar a.
\label{eq:positive-gamma-ranking}
\end{equation}
Both levels depend smoothly on $\gamma$ locally. On a possibly
smaller open neighborhood of $(\gamma,\mu)=(0,1/10)$ with
$\gamma>0$, the overprovision gap also increases with $\mu$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At $\gamma=0$, the full policy square has uniform strict
interiority margins for both private readiness $r$ and the
Bayesian location probability $p$. The analytic conditions in
Proposition~\ref{prop:global-primitive-band} also give strict
negative definiteness of the coordinated Hessian and strict
own-policy concavity for local governments, uniformly across
the compact policy square.

The differentiated Cournot payoffs and the backup first-order
system \eqref{eq:backup-linear-system} are smooth in $\gamma$ near
zero. Their denominator is $k^2$ at $\gamma=0$, uniformly over
all policy profiles and location configurations. Furthermore,
the clipped backup best-response map is a contraction with
modulus $\Delta(\gamma)J/k$, which vanishes at $\gamma=0$.
Uniform continuity on the compact policy square therefore
ensures a unique, strictly interior, smooth private-readiness
continuation for every sufficiently small $\gamma>0$.

At $\gamma=0$, the location best-response cutoffs $u_0$
and $u_1$ in \eqref{eq:location-endpoints} coincide and
are uniformly inside $(0,1)$ for every policy profile.
Smoothness of the full profile-specific continuation
profits implies that both cutoffs remain interior for
sufficiently small $\gamma>0$, uniformly in first-stage policies.
Because $|u_1-u_0|<1$, the location best response remains a
contraction on $[0,1]$, including when asymmetric location
probabilities are allowed. The unique interior Bayesian
location continuation \eqref{eq:location-prob} is therefore
smooth in policies and $\gamma$.

The resulting $\mathcal S$, $W$, and $G_j$ are $C^2$ in the
policies and $\gamma$ near the whole compact policy square.
By continuity of Hessians and strict compact-domain margins,
the globally negative-definite planner Hessian and strictly
negative government own-policy curvature persist for all
sufficiently small positive $\gamma$.

At $\gamma=0,\mu=1/10$, the augmented planner's symmetric
stationary point and the symmetric government best-response
stationary point are distinct, strictly positive, and interior.
Both derivatives defining these points have strictly negative
slopes, so the implicit function theorem continues the
two stationary levels smoothly to $\gamma>0$.
Strict global concavity makes the planner point the unique
global optimum and the government point a global unilateral
best response; symmetry establishes Nash equilibrium.
The strict gap $a^N(0)>a^P(0)$ survives by continuity.

Finally, the strict comparative static in
\eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope} holds at $(0,1/10)$.
Smooth parameter dependence preserves its sign on a
sufficiently small open neighborhood, including $\gamma>0$.
\end{proof}

The proposition is a genuine differentiated-product result,
but is deliberately local to a nonempty positive-$\gamma$
neighborhood; it does not assert the same augmented ranking
for all $\gamma\in(0,1)$ or for the original
$\gamma=12/25$ witness. The economic content is not that
generic fiscal competition or public--private substitution
is novel. Rather, the joint private-continuation game admits
a globally verified policy-ranking conflict and a
nontrivial benefit-dependent comparative static in a
primitive region that persists under interacting oligopolists.
