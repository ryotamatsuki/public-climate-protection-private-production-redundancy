\section{A Primitive Region and a Differentiated-Product Extension}
\label{sec:analytic-region}

The global certificates above establish the principal policy conflict at
explicit parameter vectors. The separable-product case also permits an
analytical sufficient-condition region. More importantly, this region
permits a continuation argument establishing a positive coordinated
optimum with decentralized overprovision for genuinely positive product
substitutability.

For $\gamma=0$, let $x=a_A$, $y=a_B$, $d=x-y$,
$q=q_0-(x+y)/2$, and set
\begin{equation}
U(q)=\frac{1}{32k}+\frac{(4k-q)(3k-q)}{128Hk^2},
\qquad h(q)=\frac{4k-q}{16k}.
\label{eq:analytic-U}
\end{equation}
Writing $t(q)=3(1-q)/8+q^2/(16k)$, the complete private
continuation induces the \emph{global} identities
\begin{equation}
\mathcal S(x,y)=2t(q)+U(q)d^2,\qquad
p(x,y)=\frac12+\frac{h(q)d}{2H}.
\label{eq:analytic-global}
\end{equation}
Let $Q=[q_0-\bar a,q_0]$, and define
\[
U_-=\min_Q U,\quad U_+=\max_Q U,\quad
D=\max_Q|U'|,\quad D_2=(64Hk^2)^{-1}.
\]
Also define
\begin{equation}
m=\frac{H(3k-q_0)+b(4k-q_0)}{16Hk},\quad
s=c-\frac{H+b}{16Hk},\quad
t_c=c-\frac1{8k},\quad
\mu_0=\frac{q_0-3k}{8k}.
\label{eq:analytic-parameters}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Global primitive conditions and benefit-induced overprovision]
\label{prop:global-primitive-band}
Suppose the following strict conditions hold:
\begin{align}
&3k<q_0<4k,\quad 0<\bar a<q_0,\quad U_->0,\quad
(4k-q_0+\bar a)\bar a<16kH,
\label{eq:analytic-band-a}\\
&\mu_0>\bar a\max\left\{0,\frac1{16k}-2U_-\right\}
+\frac{D\bar a^2}{2},
\label{eq:analytic-band-b}\\
&c>\max\left\{
\frac{1}{8k}+4U_++2D\bar a+\frac{D_2\bar a^2}{2},
\frac12\left(\frac1{8k}+2U_++2D\bar a+
\frac{D_2\bar a^2}{4}\right)+\frac{b}{16Hk}
\right\},
\label{eq:analytic-band-c}\\
&m>0,\qquad 0<m/s<\bar a.
\label{eq:analytic-band-d}
\end{align}
Then the unaugmented game has a unique coordinated global optimum
$(0,0)$ and a strictly positive symmetric decentralized Nash
equilibrium $(m/s,m/s)$, with globally optimal unilateral responses
and unique interior private continuations throughout the policy square.

If additionally $b>H$ and
\begin{equation}
\mu_0<\mu<
\min\{\mu_0+t_c\bar a,\ s\bar a-m\},
\label{eq:analytic-mu-range}
\end{equation}
then the augmented-benefit game of
\eqref{eq:augmented-direct-benefit} has a strictly positive unique
coordinated optimum and a strictly larger symmetric Nash level,
both interior and globally incentive-compatible:
\begin{equation}
a^P=\frac{\mu-\mu_0}{t_c}
<a^N=\frac{\mu+m}{s}.
\label{eq:analytic-policy-pair}
\end{equation}
The overprovision gap increases with the directly valued benefit:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial(a^N-a^P)}{\partial\mu}
=\frac{b-H}{16Hk\,s\,t_c}>0.
\label{eq:analytic-gap-slope}
\end{equation}
This is a nonempty strict primitive region, containing the exact
rational benchmark \eqref{eq:zero-delta-witness} at $\mu=1/10$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:analytic-global} follows by substituting
$r(q)=q/(4k)$ and the location profit difference
$f(q_A)-f(q_B)=h(q)(x-y)$ into the policy objectives.
The first strict inequalities imply interior, unique private
continuations over the full policy square.
Direct differentiation gives
\begin{align*}
\mathcal S_{xx}
&=\frac1{16k}+2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4}-2U'd,\\
\mathcal S_{xy}
&=\frac1{16k}-2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4},\\
\mathcal S_{yy}
&=\frac1{16k}+2U+\frac{D_2d^2}{4}+2U'd.
\end{align*}
The first cost bound implies strict negative diagonal dominance
of the symmetric Hessian $\nabla^2 W=\nabla^2\mathcal S-cI$
everywhere on the policy square, hence strict concavity.
At any $(0,y)$ the remaining marginal satisfies
\[
W_x(0,y)
=-\mu_0+\frac{y}{16k}
-\tfrac12U'(q_0-y/2)y^2-2U(q_0-y/2)y
\le-\mu_0+
\bar a\max\left\{0,\frac1{16k}-2U_-\right\}
+\frac{D\bar a^2}{2}<0.
\]
Because $W_{xx}<0$, $W_x(x,y)<0$ for $x>0$; the
symmetric argument applies to $W_y$. Thus $(0,0)$ is the
unique global coordinated optimum.

The second cost bound ensures $G_{A,xx}<0$ everywhere, because
$\partial^2(2bp)/\partial x^2=b/(16Hk)$.
At $(x,y)=(a,a)$, direct differentiation yields
$G_{A,x}=m-sa$. Condition~\eqref{eq:analytic-band-d}
places $m/s$ in the interior; strict own-policy concavity proves
it is a global best response, and symmetry gives Nash equilibrium.

Adding $\mu(x+y)$ changes no Hessian or private continuation.
At symmetry, the planner marginal becomes
$W_x(a,a)+\mu=\mu-\mu_0-t_ca$ and the local marginal becomes
$m+\mu-sa$. Their unique interior roots are
\eqref{eq:analytic-policy-pair}, and the same strict global
concavity establishes their global optimality.
At $\mu=\mu_0$, the planner root is zero and the local root
is positive. Moreover, $1/s-1/t_c$ equals the positive expression
in \eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope} when $b>H$.
Thus the local optimum strictly exceeds the coordinated level
throughout the stated interval. At the rational witness,
\[
U_-=25/128,\quad U_+=325/512,\quad
D=1875/128,\quad D_2=15625/64.
\]
The sufficient cost bounds are $925/128$ and $3625/512$,
both below $c=10$. Also $\mu_0=3/32>5/64=
\bar a\max\{0,1/(16k)-2U_-\}+D\bar a^2/2$,
the location-interiority bound is $1/160<H=1/100$,
and $m/s=1/126$. All inequalities are strict.
\end{proof}


\subsection{An open region with positive product substitutability}

\begin{proposition}[Positive optimal protection and overprovision at $\gamma>0$]
\label{prop:positive-gamma-extension}
Fix the rational primitives of \eqref{eq:zero-delta-witness} and
let $\mu=1/10$. There exists $\epsilon>0$ such that for every
$\gamma\in(0,\epsilon)$, with product substitutability strictly
positive, the complete game admits unique interior private backup
and Bayesian location continuations for all first-stage policy
profiles. The augmented coordinated protection-policy planner has a
strictly positive, symmetric \emph{unique global optimum}
$a^P(\gamma)$. The decentralized game has a strictly positive
symmetric Nash equilibrium $a^N(\gamma)$, with globally optimal
unilateral responses, satisfying
\begin{equation}
0<a^P(\gamma)<a^N(\gamma)<\bar a.
\label{eq:positive-gamma-ranking}
\end{equation}
Both levels depend smoothly on $\gamma$ locally. On a possibly
smaller open neighborhood of $(\gamma,\mu)=(0,1/10)$ with
$\gamma>0$, the overprovision gap also increases with $\mu$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At $\gamma=0$, the full policy square has uniform strict
interiority margins for both private readiness $r$ and the
Bayesian location probability $p$. The analytic conditions in
Proposition~\ref{prop:global-primitive-band} also give strict
negative definiteness of the coordinated Hessian and strict
own-policy concavity for local governments, uniformly across
the compact policy square.

The differentiated Cournot payoffs and the backup first-order
system \eqref{eq:backup-linear-system} are smooth in $\gamma$ near
zero. Their denominator is $k^2$ at $\gamma=0$, uniformly over
all policy profiles and location configurations. Furthermore,
the clipped backup best-response map is a contraction with
modulus $\Delta(\gamma)J/k$, which vanishes at $\gamma=0$.
Uniform continuity on the compact policy square therefore
ensures a unique, strictly interior, smooth private-readiness
continuation for every sufficiently small $\gamma>0$.

At $\gamma=0$, the location best-response cutoffs $u_0$
and $u_1$ in \eqref{eq:location-endpoints} coincide and
are uniformly inside $(0,1)$ for every policy profile.
Smoothness of the full profile-specific continuation
profits implies that both cutoffs remain interior for
sufficiently small $\gamma>0$, uniformly in first-stage policies.
Because $|u_1-u_0|<1$, the location best response remains a
contraction on $[0,1]$, including when asymmetric location
probabilities are allowed. The unique interior Bayesian
location continuation \eqref{eq:location-prob} is therefore
smooth in policies and $\gamma$.

The resulting $\mathcal S$, $W$, and $G_j$ are $C^2$ in the
policies and $\gamma$ near the whole compact policy square.
By continuity of Hessians and strict compact-domain margins,
the globally negative-definite planner Hessian and strictly
negative government own-policy curvature persist for all
sufficiently small positive $\gamma$.

At $\gamma=0,\mu=1/10$, the augmented planner's symmetric
stationary point and the symmetric government best-response
stationary point are distinct, strictly positive, and interior.
Both derivatives defining these points have strictly negative
slopes, so the implicit function theorem continues the
two stationary levels smoothly to $\gamma>0$.
Strict global concavity makes the planner point the unique
global optimum and the government point a global unilateral
best response; symmetry establishes Nash equilibrium.
The strict gap $a^N(0)>a^P(0)$ survives by continuity.

Finally, the strict comparative static in
\eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope} holds at $(0,1/10)$.
Smooth parameter dependence preserves its sign on a
sufficiently small open neighborhood, including $\gamma>0$.
\end{proof}

The proposition is a genuine differentiated-product result,
but is deliberately local to a nonempty positive-$\gamma$
neighborhood; it does not assert the same augmented ranking
for all $\gamma\in(0,1)$ or for the original
$\gamma=12/25$ witness. The economic content is not that
generic fiscal competition or public--private substitution
is novel. Rather, the joint private-continuation game admits
a globally verified policy-ranking conflict and a
nontrivial benefit-dependent comparative static in a
primitive region that persists under interacting oligopolists.


\subsection{Which private response drives the benefit-gap comparative static?}

The positive derivative in
\eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope} need not follow from
interjurisdictional competition alone. The two restricted
games in Proposition~\ref{prop:restricted-game-comparisons}
make it possible to separate the roles of backup and
primary-location responses while increasing the same
directly valued local benefit in both governments and the planner.

\begin{proposition}[A response-interaction test for the overprovision gap]
\label{prop:benefit-gap-response-interaction}
Hold the separable-product benchmark primitives at
\eqref{eq:zero-delta-witness}, and let
$3/32<\mu<1/8$. For each of the following
three games, augment every jurisdiction's payoff by
$\mu a_j$ and the planner's objective by
$\mu(a_A+a_B)$, holding the relevant restricted
private continuation fixed under all first-stage deviations.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item With both private backup readiness and primary-location
choice endogenous, the unique positive global coordinated
optimum and a positive symmetric globally incentive-compatible
local Nash equilibrium satisfy
\[
a^P=\frac{32\mu-3}{270},\qquad
a^N=\frac{128\mu+5}{630},\qquad
\frac{d(a^N-a^P)}{d\mu}=\frac{16}{189}>0.
\]
\item If readiness is held at the zero-protection
private level $r^*=15/16$ throughout the policy game,
while primary locations are re-solved, then
\[
a^{P,F}=\frac{\mu+3/128}{10},\qquad
a^{N,F}=\frac{\mu+25/256}{10},\qquad
\frac{d(a^{N,F}-a^{P,F})}{d\mu}=0.
\]
\item If the primary-location distribution is held at
$p=1/2$ throughout the policy game while backup
readiness is re-solved, then
\[
a^{P,L}=\frac{16}{135}\left(\mu-\frac3{32}\right),
\quad
a^{N,L}=\frac{32}{295}\left(\mu-\frac3{64}\right),
\quad
\frac{d(a^{N,L}-a^{P,L})}{d\mu}
=-\frac{16}{1593}<0.
\]
\end{enumerate}

All stated coordinated levels are unique global optima;
all local Nash levels are strictly positive, interior,
and globally optimal against unilateral deviations.
Thus, in this exact benchmark, the positive response of
the local overprovision gap to a common direct protection
benefit requires the joint endogenous operation of
readiness and location. This is a \emph{within-model
counterfactual necessity result}, not a claim that both
responses are necessary for overprovision in general.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Part (i) follows from Proposition~
\ref{prop:positive-optimum-benchmark} and the exact
positive-gap derivative in
\eqref{eq:analytic-gap-slope}, evaluated at the
maintained rational parameters.

For part (ii), the fixed-readiness continuation in
Proposition~\ref{prop:restricted-game-comparisons}
leaves each government's own-policy curvature equal
to $-10$ along the symmetric policy path, identical
to the planner's symmetric own-policy curvature.
The new linear benefit simply adds $\mu$ to each
first-order condition; thus the planner and local
stationary roots both rise at rate $1/10$.
The full-square planner Hessian remains strictly
negative definite, and each government's own-policy
second derivative is $-20405/2048<0$.
The roots therefore remain globally optimal.

For part (iii), the previously re-solved fixed-location
game has the exact partial derivatives
$W_{a_A}=-3/32-(135/16)a_A$ and
$G_{A,a_A}=-3/64-(295/32)a_A$.
Adding $\mu$ yields the roots displayed above.
Each objective is strictly concave in the relevant
policy coordinates over the entire policy square.
On the specified open interval, all six stated
policy levels are strictly between $0$ and
$\bar a=2/25$. Exact subtraction gives
$32/295-16/135=-16/1593$.
The independent exact scripts
\texttt{positive\_optimum\_exact.py} and
\texttt{restricted\_games\_exact.py}
regenerate the resulting comparisons.
\end{proof}

The contrasts sharpen the contribution of the
benefit-gap result. A policy-directed direct benefit
changes all three models in the same direction.
When private redundancy is fixed, the planner and
local choice move in parallel and the overprovision
gap does not grow. When firms are geographically
immobile, the gap shrinks as the direct benefit
rises. With both channels present, the same
benefit increase widens the gap, and Proposition~
\ref{prop:positive-gamma-extension} establishes
persistence of this positive-gap result for a
nonempty set of differentiated-product economies.


\subsection{Robustness to regional incidence and availability-dependent hosting}
\label{sec:incidence-robustness}

The baseline divides industrial market surplus equally between
jurisdictions and values hosting an established primary plant
irrespective of disaster-state production. Both assumptions can
be relaxed locally without changing private production decisions.
Let
\begin{equation}
\theta_A(p)=\frac12+\eta\left(p-\frac12\right),
\qquad \theta_B(p)=1-\theta_A(p),
\label{eq:endogenous-incidence}
\end{equation}
where $|\eta|<1$ ensures both shares are nonnegative whenever
$p\in[0,1]$. Positive $\eta$ reallocates more industrial surplus
to the jurisdiction attracting primary facilities. Separately,
let $\rho\in[0,1]$ denote the fraction of the location-attached
benefit that requires continued production availability.

Denote by $r_1^{XY}$ firm 1's unique backup choice when its
primary plant is in $X$ and its rival's plant is in $Y$.
Define the expected operating probabilities conditional on the
firm's primary location:
\begin{align}
v_A&=p\{1-q_A(1-r_1^{AA})\}
 +(1-p)\{1-q_A(1-r_1^{AB})\},\\
v_B&=p\{1-q_B(1-r_1^{BA})\}
 +(1-p)\{1-q_B(1-r_1^{BB})\}.
\label{eq:availability-conditional}
\end{align}
These formulas use the original joint-failure and conditional
backup-success law. They do not introduce new private actions.
The local objectives become
\begin{align}
G_A^{\eta,\rho}
 &=\theta_A(p)\mathcal S
   +2bp[(1-\rho)+\rho v_A]
   -\frac{c}{2}a_A^2+\mu a_A,\\
G_B^{\eta,\rho}
 &=\theta_B(p)\mathcal S
   +2b(1-p)[(1-\rho)+\rho v_B]
   -\frac{c}{2}a_B^2+\mu a_B,
\label{eq:perturbed-regional-payoffs}
\end{align}
and the coordinated planner continues to maximize their sum
$W^{\rho}=G_A^{\eta,\rho}+G_B^{\eta,\rho}$.
The planner's objective is independent of $\eta$ by construction
but now values expected operating-related hosting benefits
when $\rho>0$. The original model is exactly the case
$(\eta,\rho)=(0,0)$.

\begin{proposition}[Robustness to two economic incidence assumptions]
\label{prop:incidence-robustness}
Fix $(q_0,k,H,b,c,\bar a)=(3/10,2/25,1/100,11/200,10,2/25)$,
and take $(\gamma,\eta,\rho,\mu)=(0,0,0,1/10)$
as the reference point.
There exists $\varepsilon>0$ such that for every
\[
0\le\gamma<\varepsilon,\quad
|\eta|<\varepsilon,\quad
0\le\rho<\varepsilon,\quad
|\mu-1/10|<\varepsilon
\]
within the maintained feasible parameter domain, the
perturbed game has unique interior firm-level backup and
Bayesian location continuations at every public-policy profile.
Its coordinated policy optimum is unique, symmetric, strictly
positive, and interior. Its decentralized policy game has a
strictly positive symmetric Nash equilibrium at which each
jurisdiction's first-stage policy choice is a \emph{global}
best response; that Nash provision strictly exceeds the
coordinated level. Moreover, the overprovision gap continues
to increase strictly with the common direct protection benefit
$\mu$ throughout this neighborhood.

At the separable-product reference point
$(\gamma,\rho,\eta)=(0,0,0)$, the effect of strengthening
the location-dependence of regional industrial surplus incidence
is strictly positive:
\begin{equation}
\left.
\frac{\partial a^N}{\partial\eta}
\right|_{\mu=1/10,\,\gamma=\rho=\eta=0}
=\frac{t(q^N)h(q^N)}{H s}
=\frac{19956004}{78764805}>0,
\qquad q^N=q_0-\frac{89}{3150}.
\label{eq:incidence-response}
\end{equation}
This is an equilibrium comparative static, not just an
on-path marginal effect.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At $(\gamma,\eta,\rho,\mu)=(0,0,0,1/10)$,
Propositions~\ref{prop:positive-optimum-benchmark},
\ref{prop:global-primitive-band}, and
\ref{prop:positive-gamma-extension} give uniform strict
continuation-interiority margins on the compact policy square,
a strictly negative-definite Hessian for the coordinated
planner, strict local own-policy concavity, two nondegenerate
strictly positive symmetric policy roots, a strict
decentralized--coordinated ranking, and a strictly positive
derivative of the policy-level gap with respect to $\mu$.

The location-conditional availability functions in
\eqref{eq:availability-conditional} are finite weighted sums
of the original profile-specific readiness functions and the
Bayesian cutoff probability. All are smooth in public
policies and $\gamma$ in a neighborhood of the compact
policy square by the nonsingular private backup system and
uniformly interior location cutoffs. The new shares and
hosting payoffs are polynomial in $\eta,\rho$ and smooth
in the same private continuations. Thus the modified local
payoffs and their sum are jointly $C^2$ in public policies
and parameters, with continuous higher derivatives needed
for the policy-level comparative static. Uniform strict
Hessian and interior margins therefore persist for
sufficiently small perturbations. The implicit function
theorem continues the positive symmetric planner and Nash
policy levels smoothly in $(\gamma,\eta,\rho,\mu)$.
Strict global planner concavity and strict local own-policy
concavity establish full-policy optimality, while
continuous dependence preserves the positive gap and its
strictly positive $\mu$ derivative.

For the final formula, set $\gamma=\rho=0$. On the
symmetric path,
\[
\frac{\partial G_A^{\eta,0}}{\partial a_A}(a,a)
=m+\mu-sa+\frac{\eta}{H}\,
 t(q_0-a)\,h(q_0-a),
\]
where $m,s$ are defined by \eqref{eq:analytic-parameters}.
At $\eta=0$, the equilibrium root is
$a^N=(\mu+m)/s$, and its derivative with
respect to the same common policy level is $-s<0$.
Implicit differentiation gives
$\partial_\eta a^N=t(q^N)h(q^N)/(Hs)$.
Substitution of the rational witness yields
\eqref{eq:incidence-response}. The coordinated planner's
policy is independent of $\eta$, since its two regional
surplus shares always sum to one.
\end{proof}

The robustness claim remains local: it does not deliver
a global characterization for large reallocations of
industrial surplus or fully availability-contingent hosting.
It does establish that neither an exactly fixed half-share
incidence rule nor fully state-independent hosting is
necessary for the positive-optimum policy ranking or
benefit-gap comparative static in an open region of
differentiated-product economies.
